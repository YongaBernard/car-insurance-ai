CAR INSURANCE POLICY
Policy Type: Comprehensive Motor Insurance

1. ACCIDENTAL DAMAGE COVER

The policy provides cover for accidental physical damage to the insured vehicle resulting from a collision, impact, overturning, or other sudden accidental event.

2. THIRD-PARTY COLLISION

Damage resulting from a collision with another vehicle or object may be covered, subject to the terms, conditions, excess, and exclusions of the policy.

3. THEFT AND VANDALISM

The policy provides cover for loss or damage resulting from theft, attempted theft, or malicious damage, subject to applicable policy conditions.

4. FIRE DAMAGE

Damage caused directly by accidental fire may be covered under the policy.

5. EXCLUSIONS

The policy does not normally cover:

- Deliberate damage caused by the policyholder.
- Damage resulting from illegal use of the vehicle.
- Damage occurring while the driver is under the influence of alcohol or prohibited substances.
- Normal wear and tear.
- Mechanical failure that is not caused by an insured accident.
- Damage that existed before the reported incident.

6. DRIVER REQUIREMENTS

The vehicle must be driven by an authorised driver who holds a valid driving licence and complies with the conditions of the policy.

7. CLAIM NOTIFICATION

The policyholder should notify the insurer of an accident as soon as reasonably practicable and provide accurate information about the incident.

8. EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

The insurer may request:

- Photographs of vehicle damage.
- Accident descriptions.
- Repair estimates.
- Police reports where applicable.
- Witness information.
- Other documentation reasonably required to assess the claim.

9. EXCESS

The policyholder may be required to pay the applicable policy excess before an eligible claim is settled.

10. CLAIM ASSESSMENT

Coverage is subject to verification of the policy terms, circumstances of the incident, available evidence, applicable exclusions, and any other relevant conditions.

11. HUMAN REVIEW

AI-generated assessments are decision-support tools only. Final insurance decisions must be reviewed and authorised by an appropriately qualified human insurance professional.

12. FRAUD AND INCONSISTENCY REVIEW

Claims may require additional investigation where the available evidence contains significant inconsistencies, unusual circumstances, conflicting information, or indications of potential fraud.

13. IMPORTANT LIMITATION

The presence of damage in a photograph does not by itself establish policy coverage, liability, causation, or the final amount payable.
